## Anti-Patterns

<!-- Document approaches that DON'T work, to avoid repeating them -->

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails | Better Approach |
|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Changing multiple things at once | Can't tell which change fixed it | One change at a time |
| Ignoring warnings | Warnings often predict errors | Address warnings early |
| Copy-pasting fixes from internet | May not match your context | Understand the fix first |
| Disabling error checks | Hides the problem | Fix the underlying issue |
